# 08: Renewals

**What to build:** Some open Deals are Renewals of existing Retainers. Maya can tell them apart on the board, and winning one extends the same Retainer instead of creating a second one.

**Blocked by:** 07 (Won and Retainers).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Deal has a kind, New business or Renewal. A Renewal belongs to the Retainer it renews.
- [ ] The Template includes 5 open Renewal Deals for Retainers ending soon.
- [ ] Renewal cards show a "Renewal · " prefix, and the Deal Sheet header shows New business or Renewal.
- [ ] Maya can create a Renewal Deal from a Retainer.
- [ ] Winning a Renewal (same Won rules and confirmation) updates the existing Retainer's end date and monthly fee from the Proposal and logs an Activity, in one transaction. No second Retainer is created.
