# 03: Companies, Contacts and Activities

**What to build:** Maya can find a Company in the Companies list, open its page, see its Contacts and Activity timeline, keep Companies and Contacts up to date, and log an Activity. The Template grows to the full set of Companies, Contacts and Activities.

**Blocked by:** 02 (Walking skeleton).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Template holds about 40 Companies, 80 Contacts and the Activities that belong to them, all fake and checked for clashes with real businesses, with dates relative to the anchor.
- [ ] The Companies list has a text filter and an Owner filter. The Client or Prospect filter is present but treats every Company as a Prospect until ticket 07 adds Retainers.
- [ ] The Company page shows its Contacts and its Activity timeline (kind icon, author, kind, when, text), using Variant H's cards and tables.
- [ ] Maya can create, edit and delete Companies and Contacts.
- [ ] Maya can log an Activity (Email, Call, Meeting or Note) with a date and text against a Company, optionally with a Contact.
- [ ] Contacts and Activities are covered by the isolation test.
- [ ] Every screen in this ticket meets the spec's accessibility rules (visible labels, focus ring, 44px targets, nothing under 12px).
