# 09: Lost and closed Deals

**What to build:** Maya marks a Deal Lost and has to say why. Lost Deals show their reason, and an Open / Closed switch on the Pipeline shows Won and Lost Deals in a simple table, so the board itself stays about live work.

**Blocked by:** 05 (Pipeline board and Deal panel).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Moving a Deal to Lost (dropdown or "Move to...") asks for a Lost reason: Price, Timing, Chose another agency, Went in-house, No response or Other. The CRM module returns the typed "Lost reason required" result without one.
- [ ] A Lost Deal's Sheet shows its Lost reason in a red-tinted banner with the words, not color alone.
- [ ] Won and Lost Deals never appear on the grid.
- [ ] An Open / Closed switch beside Mine / Everyone shows Closed Deals in a table (Company, Deal, Owner, Stage, Monthly value, Lost reason, closed date). Each row opens the Deal Sheet.
- [ ] The Template includes some Lost Deals with a spread of Lost reasons and some Won Deals, dated over recent months.
